Creator tips

Make your livestream more discoverable and reliable.

Make your title clear

Use a short, descriptive title that tells viewers what is happening.

Choose the right category

Help viewers discover your broadcast by selecting an appropriate category.

Protect your stream credentials

Never share your OBS stream key. Anyone with access may be able to broadcast to your stream.

Keep your account secure

StreamSphere will never ask you to share your password or private broadcasting credentials with other users.
